The flow turns Tiny Breakout's SystemVerilog source, the RTL, into the layout of one tile of a chip. Synthesis maps the RTL to a gate netlist: sky130 standard cells and the nets between them, with no positions. Place and route gives every cell a position on the die, adds cells such as buffers and the clock tree, and draws every wire. Sign-off extracts the wires' resistance and capacitance and checks timing, writes the layout as GDS, and checks its shapes against the design rules and its circuit against the gate netlist. Tiny Tapeout checks the finished tile again and puts it on a shuttle chip with many other designs.

The chart is wider than the screen; scroll it sideways.
